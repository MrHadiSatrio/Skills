# Report Formats

The reports converged over many sessions. Keep their shapes, so the
athlete reads each report the same way every time.

## 1. The post-run debrief

Sections, in order:

1. **Verdict** — the whole-run verdict first, in one or two
   sentences. Scope every caveat to its component: a caveat that
   opens the report reads as a disqualification of the run. When a
   run partly met its aim, say how much — "a true long run, with
   perhaps 90% of the ideal stimulus" beats a list of deductions.
2. **The prescription scorecard** — a table: each prescription
   element against its outcome.
3. **The stop timeline** — from `coach stops`, with each stop
   classified from the athlete's debrief answers.
4. **Zone discipline** — the zone record under the frame that governs
   (the live Garmin band), lap by lap where it matters.
5. **Drift and weather** — decoupling with dew point and the
   start-time class named.
6. **Body, fuel, and perception** — sleep, HRV, RHR, readiness, RPE
   against feel, caffeine, and intake against the fuel plan.
7. **Watch items** — what tonight or the next run must answer.
8. **Records and gaps** — every data point that was pulled and every
   lead that stayed blocked, tool errors included. Never drop a gap
   without a word.

State deltas with the numbers on the two sides: "48 s/km slower at 4
bpm HIGHER HR" beats "slower and harder".

## 2. The pre-run consult

When the athlete asks what to do with a constrained day:

1. **Option 1 (recommended)** — with the reason.
2. **Option 2** — the honest alternative, usually rest.
3. **What not to do** — the tempting error, as a CAUTION with the
   specific risk.

Name the three votes on every go/no-go answer: the plan's vote (the
metrics), the coach's vote (the judgment), and the body's vote (the
morning gate, with its named condition). Time-capped days run for
time: the distance is an output, not a target.

## 3. The interrogation

Gather ALL data before any question. Then ask once: one batched
AskUserQuestion, at most four questions, each with the evidence in
the question and concrete hypotheses as options. Ask, do not assume —
a guessed fact that reaches the journal costs a correction later.
Every open ruling in `rulings.md` that the day touched becomes one of
the questions. If AskUserQuestion is unavailable, ask the same batch
in plain text.
